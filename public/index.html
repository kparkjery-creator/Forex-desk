What moves gold

  • Statement wording first — changes vs prior meeting matter more than a priced hold
  • Hike → USD up / sell gold pressure
  • Hold + hawkish (sticky inflation, higher-for-longer) → sell gold lean
  • Hold + neutral → often fade the initial spike; wait for presser
  • Dovish hold / cut talk → buy gold path if price gate confirms

Desk rules

  • FLAT into major releases and the final hour before decisions
  • Trade the surprise vs. expectations, not the headline alone
  • Press conferences can reverse the first 15–30 min reaction
  • Price gate required: gold + USD/yields must stop fighting each other
  • Always size by stop distance under your 1% risk cap
