What moves gold

  • Statement wording first — changes vs Sept matter more than a priced hold
  • Hike → USD up / sell gold
  • Hold + hawkish (higher for longer, inflation risks) → sell gold lean
  • Hold + neutral → often fade the first spike; wait for presser
  • Dovish hold / cut talk → buy gold path if price agrees

Desk rules

  • FLAT into the last 2 hours before 20:00 CAT
  • Trade the statement surprise, not the rate alone
  • Press conference can reverse the first 15–30 min move
  • Price gate required: gold + DXY + yields must agree
  • Hot Core CPI (0.3%) already leans sell gold into this meeting
