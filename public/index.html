How to read it

  • NFP direction is surprise vs forecast, then wages and revisions. ADP, claims, ISM employment and JOLTS are the small prints.
  • CPI and PPI: core beats the headline. Hot core = USD up, gold down.
  • FOMC follows CPI, PCE and NFP. Do not fade a hot inflation stack.
  • Do not buy gold before NFP unless wages print soft (about 0.2% or lower) and jobs miss.
