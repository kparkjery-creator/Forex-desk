EMA Trend rules

  • Ribbon — EMAs 5, 11, 15, 18, 21, 24, 28, 34. Blue stack when EMA11 above EMA34 (bullish ribbon). Gray when flipped.
  • BUY — EMA11 crosses above EMA34 (confirmed bar close). Extra EMA200 is visual only, not in the signal.
  • SELL — EMA11 crosses below EMA34 (confirmed bar close).
  • Stop — Entry ± ATR(14) × 2.0 (default).
  • Take profits — Up to 4 TPs, each step = SL distance × TP factor (default 1.0), so TP1 ≈ 1R, TP2 ≈ 2R, etc.
  • Trade end — SL hit or all TPs filled. Lines freeze at exit.
  • MTF dashboard — 5m / 15m / 30m / 1H / 4H / Daily: bullish if EMA11 > EMA34 on that TF. Combined status needs 2 of 3 selected TFs bullish.
  • Desk filter — Prefer longs only when Bias board is not in no-trade lockout and not fighting a fakeout warning. Prefer shorts when macro score is firm USD-up / sell gold.

Session separator rules (CAT)

  • Asia 00:00–08:00 — quiet range, smaller breakouts.
  • London 08:00–14:00 — liquidity builds.
  • London–NY overlap 14:00–16:00 — highest impact; US Tier-1 data often 14:30.
  • New York 14:00–22:00 — trend or fade of the data move.
  • US data killzone 14:00–15:30 — shaded red; reduce size or wait for the print + first hold.
  • Session high/low lines mark Asia and London ranges for ORB / fade plans.
